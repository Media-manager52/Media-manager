## What changed since v0.1.0-beta.18

The [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) section `0.1.0-beta.19` lists the changes
(PR #229), two defects found by a DeepSeek V4.1 Flash review and verified
against the code: commentary and audio-description tracks (and commentary
sidecars) no longer count as language evidence, and a quality profile whose
policy is `null` is refused. Language and quality requirements are unchanged
or stricter.
