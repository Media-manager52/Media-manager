## What changed since v0.1.0-beta.11

The [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) section `0.1.0-beta.12` lists the change
(PR #215): before a title from a followed MDBList list is added, it is looked
up in the owner's Plex libraries (standard-edition folders of that kind) by
its `tmdb://`, `imdb://` or, for series, `tvdb://` GUID. A title Plex already
has is recorded as `in_library` with detail `in_plex` and is not fetched
again. When Plex cannot be read the read adds nothing and is retried after an
hour, so a Plex outage cannot create duplicates. Language and quality rules are
unchanged.
