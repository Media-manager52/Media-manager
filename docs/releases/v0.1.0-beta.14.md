## What changed since v0.1.0-beta.13

The [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) section `0.1.0-beta.14` lists the ten
fixes (PR #219) from an automated review of search, indexer pacing and
downloading: AltMount failures after an NZB fetch defer by 5 minutes and the
retry reaches AltMount before the indexer; a provider `Retry-After` is capped
at the maximum backoff; incomplete searches retry quickly at most three times
in a row before following the normal schedule; waiting partial retries no
longer starve feed matches; paging completeness follows the stated total; an
episode does not reuse a season answer after its own search; only
identifiable uploads steer the feed catch-up; reused season answers keep
derived facts; held re-scores skip releases returned in the same run; and
dynamic range is read from whole tokens. Language and quality requirements
are not weakened.
