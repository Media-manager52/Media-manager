## What changed since v0.1.0-beta.17

The [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) section `0.1.0-beta.18` lists the changes
(PR #227), two defects found by a DeepSeek V4.1 Flash review and verified
against the code: subtitles whose name marks them as a sample, trailer, extra
or bonus are no longer paired with the feature (they had satisfied a required
Danish subtitle), and deleting a title recycles verified links whose Plex
confirmation never finished. Language and quality requirements are unchanged
or stricter.
