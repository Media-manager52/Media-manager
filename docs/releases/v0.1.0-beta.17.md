## What changed since v0.1.0-beta.16

The [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) section `0.1.0-beta.17` lists the changes
(PR #225), two defects found by a DeepSeek V4.1 Flash review and verified
against the code: a release failure recorded after the owner cancelled a
workflow no longer reopens it, and reviving pre-submission archive rejections
renumbers the round's remaining slots so the next submission cannot collide
after its NZB was fetched. Language and quality requirements are unchanged.
