## What changed since v0.1.0-beta.12

The [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) section `0.1.0-beta.13` lists the ten
fixes (PR #217) from an automated review of the language checks, publication
and automatic recovery: re-checks of kept downloads use the full language
policy (Danish audio included); an interrupted publication resumes its own
later revision; an unreadable mount or library folder defers publication by 5
minutes instead of failing it; un-inspectable re-checks and AltMount queue
items deleted while queued stop retrying; common three-letter track language
codes are mapped; only real sample folders and file-name tokens mark samples;
duplicate subtitle destinations publish one file; stored texts are cut on
whole characters; and automatic Plex re-confirmations no longer use up the
owner's own retries. Language and quality requirements are not weakened.
